# CS-360-Mobile-Architect-Programming
EventTracker App – Final Project Reflection
1. Briefly summarize the requirements and goals of the app you developed. What user needs was this app designed to address?
I created the EventTracker software to assist individuals stay organized and on top of important events. The software is user intuitive and allows users to create, view and manage events. I wanted to design a tool that could be used every day, to remember important events like birthdays, appointments, or whatever else. One of my main goals was to make the app so intuitive that anyone could use it, regardless of their expertise with similar tools. From this project I realized the importance of thinking about the genuine requirement of users when building an app.
2. What screens and features were necessary to support user needs and produce a user-centered UI for the app? How did your UI designs keep users in mind? Why were your designs successful?
I built three screens for my EventTracker app: one for organizing events, one for receiving SMS notifications and one for signing in. The user interface of the program has been carefully designed; each screen has its purpose. The application could be accessed through the login screen, and events could be scheduled on the event management screen. The SMS notification screen makes it easy to set important calendar reminders.
I tried to make it more basic and orderly than trying to fit too much information into each panel. I thought the blue and aqua combo made the app look more polished and modern therefore I went with that color palette. I tried to keep the buttons familiar and the layouts uniform to help users find their way around. I think my designs were successful since I made sure to have an easy-to-navigate interface with a good esthetic.
3. How did you approach the process of coding your app? What techniques or strategies did you use? How could those techniques or strategies be applied in the future?
When I began working on my EventTracker software, I understood that it would be easier to do things one step at a time, instead than trying to complete everything at once. I started with the layouts in android studio and after that I added the different features. Because I am still learning there were times, I did not know what I was doing or why things did not work. It helped to break the assignment into smaller pieces so I could comprehend things without getting too angry
It helped me a lot to review my code as I proceeded. If I had a problem I would go back and see what I had done and where I may have made a mistake. Sometimes it was something little I’d missed. I also learned that having my code organized made it much easier to make modifications when I needed to. This assignment taught me that I need to be patient with myself because I’m not going to get everything on the first try. I know I have a lot to learn and that I am becoming better with each endeavor. I intend to continue to apply what I’ve learnt as I tackle more demanding applications. Eventually I would like to design my own apps and start a software firm, so I see this as another step to that aim.
4. How did you test to ensure your code was functional? Why is this process important, and what did it reveal?
Android Studio let me see what my code looked like when the app was running, and allowed me to check for problems whilst working on my app. I carefully checked the displays to make sure they were showing up properly, and the buttons and navigation were functioning as planned. I would test the app for any problems before I would make any changes to the layout or other pieces of the app.
I have found that something can look OK in code, but not in the actual application. Sometimes when things didn’t go how I wanted them to, I had to go back and do some editing. Through testing, I was able to uncover these flaws and see where I might make improvements. Another thing I learnt is that it’s not enough to finish generating code. I also can’t assume everything working. The only way to tell if it works as, it should be to see it in action.
5. Consider the full app design and development process from initial planning to finalization. Where did you have to innovate to overcome a challenge?
The hardest part of creating my EventTracker application was getting the look I desired without reverting to an older version. I chose to tweak the app’s color palette, the arrangement of the buttons and the screen layout to make it stand out from the throng. I was new to Android Studio, so I didn’t always know how to implement specific changes. Sometimes I would make a modification and then realize something else wasn't quite right and I would have to make another adjustment.
So rather than give up or just leave the project, I opted to learn how to update the XML layouts. Sometimes I became upset, because it was pretty much trial and error.” But the extra effort paid off as the app started to take shape just how I had envisioned. The most important thing is that I learnt that it’s acceptable if I don’t have the answer right immediately. I must explore every now and then, recheck my work or repair a problem every now and then. My confidence soared as I met goals and found solutions to obstacles.
6. In what specific component of your mobile app were you particularly successful in demonstrating your knowledge, skills, and experience?
I’m extremely pleased with how the user interface for my EventTracker software has come together to its final form. I began working on the project before I really understood how to customize layouts and apply consistent design concepts. Working on it got me to a point where I could easily rearrange screens, change colors and customize buttons.
I like the turquoise and navy color mix; it really made the app look like something I made and not just a school assignment. I still have a long way to go in my coding and application development education, but I am proud of the progress I was able to document in this project. It was good to be able to take a notion and convert it into something I could see and interact with. This has inspired me to continue learning, as I aspire to create my own apps and a software company. This project will be of great help in bringing us closer to our end target.
